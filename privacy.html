Privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026. Applies to the Tula app for iPhone and Android.

The short version

The developer collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your phone. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The one exception is on Android: when you scan a label, Google's scanner sends Google some technical diagnostics (see below).

What stays on your phone

Your tarantulas, their logs, photos, documents and settings are stored in Tula's storage on your phone. The developer can't see them. Your phone's own backup can include them: iCloud Backup on iPhone, under Apple's privacy policy, and Google backup on Android, under Google's privacy policy. That is between you and Apple or Google.

Permissions Tula asks for

  • Camera and photos: to add pictures of your tarantulas and, on iPhone, to scan the QR codes on enclosure labels. Pictures are copied into Tula; nothing is uploaded.
  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled on your phone. Nothing goes through a server.
  • Calendar (only if you turn it on): Tula adds care reminders to the calendar you choose and updates or removes the events it made. It doesn't read or change your other events. If that calendar syncs to an account such as iCloud or Google, those events are handled under that service's policy.
  • Face ID, fingerprint or screen lock (only if you turn on App Lock): handled by your phone. Tula only learns whether the unlock succeeded.

Scanning labels on Android

On Android, Tula scans enclosure labels with Google's code scanner, part of Google Play services. Tula only receives the text in the code. When you use it, Google's ML Kit sends Google diagnostic and usage information: device and app details, a per-installation identifier, performance metrics and error codes. Google uses it to keep the scanner working and doesn't share it with third parties (Google's ML Kit data disclosure). None of your tarantulas' records are included. If you never scan a label, none of this is sent. On iPhone, scanning is done by iOS on the phone and nothing is sent.

Files you export

Backups, spreadsheets, care sheets, labels and share cards are created only when you ask, and go only where you send them (your files, email, a printer or another app).

Children

Tula is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
