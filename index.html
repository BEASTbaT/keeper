Tula support

Tula is an iPhone and Android app for keeping records of your tarantulas: feedings, molts, water, growth and photos, with care timing for each one's species and size.

Contact

Email xhander.hoek@gmail.com with questions, problems or species corrections. Include your phone model and the Tula version (Settings › About in Tula).

Common questions

Where is my data? On your phone only. Tula has no account and no server. Your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android) can include it, and Settings › Backup and export in Tula makes a backup file with your photos that you can keep anywhere.

Moving to a new phone? Make a backup file in Tula on the old phone and restore it on the new one (Settings › Backup and export). This also works between iPhone and Android. Between two iPhones, restoring from iCloud Backup works too.

Coming from ExotiKeeper? In Tula, go to Settings › Import › ExotiKeeper and choose your ExotiKeeper backup (the ZIP, or the .db files).

Reminders don't arrive. Check that notifications are allowed for Tula in your phone's Settings app, then open Tula once: it schedules the next two weeks each time it opens.

Where do the care values come from? Each value in a species page shows its source and a confidence grade. Species names follow the World Spider Catalog.

Species names: Natural History Museum Bern, World Spider Catalog, via Catalogue of Life (CC BY).
